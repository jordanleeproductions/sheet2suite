## 4. Short-Form Video Scripts (TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)

### Script 1: The "$89 QR Wedding Photo App" Hack (18 Seconds)
- **Visual Hook (0–3s)**: Text on screen: *"Stop paying $89 for a QR Code Wedding Photo App when you already have free Google Drive storage."*
- **Demo (3–10s)**: Show clicking **Share → Guest Photo Upload Link** inside Sheet2Vow, scanning on a phone, selecting 3 camera roll photos + typing *"Congrats Alex & Sam!"*
- **Payoff (10–15s)**: Show the photos appearing inside the couple's own Google Drive folder + inside the Sheet2Vow Guestbook tab.
- **CTA (15–18s)**: *"Included free inside the Sheet2Vow Google Sheets Wedding App on Etsy. Test the live demo in bio!"*

### Script 2: "Type-A Bride vs. Type-B Partner" (15 Seconds)
- **Visual Hook (0–3s)**: *"POV: You love planning your wedding in Google Sheets, but your fiancé gets a headache looking at rows and columns."*
- **Demo (3–11s)**: Show the 15-tab Google Sheet, then switch tabs to the Sheet2Vow Editorial Dashboard, dragging guests onto Round Table #1 and toggling Dark Mode.
- **CTA (11–15s)**: *"Both of you edit the exact same Google Sheet in real time. One-time download on Etsy!"*

### Script 3: The Caterer Allergy Breakdown Lifesaver (15 Seconds)
- **Visual Hook (0–3s)**: *"When your caterer asks 48 hours before the wedding: 'Which tables have the 4 gluten-free and 2 nut-allergy guests?'"*
- **Demo (3–11s)**: Open the Sheet2Vow **Live Vendor Share Portal → Catering View**, click **Gluten-Free**, and show it immediately expanding the exact guest names, meal choices, and table numbers.
- **CTA (11–15s)**: *"Send your caterer a live read-only link in 1 click. Link in bio!"*
